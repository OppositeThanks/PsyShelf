DEMO - Cognitive Reappraisal
Original educational sample prepared for a PsyShelf user walkthrough.
2026-10-02 | Language: English | Topic: Emotion regulation

CONCEPT
Cognitive reappraisal involves reconsidering the meaning of an event in a way that can change the emotional response. Appraisal also plays a role in how people experience stress.

EXAMPLE
Before a presentation, a student interprets a racing heart as preparation for a demanding task rather than certain evidence of failure.

KEY DISTINCTION
Reappraisal does not require denying facts or pretending a difficult situation is harmless. Its usefulness depends on context; practical action may also be needed.

READING QUESTION
What interpretation changed, and did the facts of the situation change?
