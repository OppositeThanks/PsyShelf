DEMO - Cognitive Dissonance
Original educational sample prepared for a PsyShelf user walkthrough.
2026-10-02 | Language: English | Topic: Social psychology

CONCEPT
Cognitive dissonance is discomfort associated with inconsistency between beliefs, attitudes or behavior. People may reduce that discomfort by changing behavior, revising a belief, or adding a justification.

EXAMPLE
A person values environmental protection but repeatedly buys disposable products. They might change their purchasing habits or justify the purchases as unavoidable.

KEY DISTINCTION
A justification may reduce discomfort without resolving the underlying inconsistency. The concept describes a psychological process rather than proving why any particular person acted.

READING QUESTION
What is inconsistent, and what strategy is used to reduce the discomfort?
